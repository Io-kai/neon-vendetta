# NEON VENDETTA — Replacement Narrative

## 1. Premise

Korvo choked the district's power core to force every bar, arcade, and back-alley clinic to pay his cut. When the heroes take him down, the core reboots, the neon comes back block by block, and nobody in the district owes Korvo a cent again.

That's the whole engine. No factions, no double-crosses, no companions required to understand it — just three people walking into the dark to turn the lights back on.

---

## 2. Cinematic Scenes

All scenes advance automatically, support manual advance, and can be skipped. Speaker lines work unmodified for any of the three heroes — no per-hero variants.

### OPENING — "Lights Out" (3 shots, 22 words)

**Shot 1 (4s)** — Wide establishing shot of the district: neon signs guttering out one by one down the street, animated flicker-to-black on each sign, the generator tower glowing an unhealthy red in the distance.
On-screen text: *"THE DISTRICT WENT DARK LAST NIGHT."*

**Shot 2 (4s)** — Selected hero sprite walks in from the one still-lit doorway, passing a sparking jukebox (simple animated prop, spark loop).
Speaker: **HERO**
Subtitle: *"Korvo pulls the plug, everybody pays. Not tonight."*

**Shot 3 (3s)** — Two Punk enemies block an alley mouth under a hand-painted sign that flickers between lit and dark: "PAY OR STAY DARK" (animated prop, alternates every ~1s).
Speaker: **PUNK**
Subtitle: *"Turn around. Korvo's collecting."*

### MIDPOINT — "The Core" (1 shot, 6 words)

**Shot (5s)** — Industrial generator room. Korvo stands beside the sputtering core (animated prop: core pulses irregularly, casting shifting light across his silhouette), hero framed on the far side of the room.
Speaker: **KORVO**
Subtitle: *"Break me, or stay dark forever."*

### ENDING — "Back On" (2 shots, 15 words)

**Shot 1 (3s)** — The core stabilizes and flares to steady white-blue (animated prop: sputter resolves into a clean, held glow), light visibly spreading outward through a window to the street beyond.
On-screen text: *"CORE ONLINE. THE DISTRICT LIGHTS UP."*

**Shot 2 (4s)** — Hero steps out into the street as the club marquee overhead ignites (animated prop: marquee bulbs light in sequence), a small crowd sprite cheering in the background.
Speaker: **HERO**
Subtitle: *"Korvo's out of the dark. Drinks are on him."*

## 3. Character Dossiers (Optional)

**KANE — Balanced Brawler**
Used to work security for men like Korvo. Quit when the job stopped being about safety and started being about leverage. Fights clean, talks less, finishes what he starts.

**JINX — Fast Courier**
Runs the district's back routes faster than anyone alive or dead. Doesn't care about Korvo's politics — cares that half her regular stops just went dark. Fixes that the direct way.

**BULL — Heavy Fighter**
Was here the night the reactor lit the sky orange. Never left. The district raised him after that; he's not about to watch Korvo starve it for spare change.

**KORVO — Boss**
Built his empire on scarcity, not product. Doesn't sell power — sells the absence of it, one district at a time, to whoever's desperate enough to keep paying.

## 4. Lore Fragments (Optional, none required for objective clarity)

1. **THE BLACKOUT PROTOCOL** — Korvo's crews cut a block's feed, wait a week, then offer to "restore priority service" for a fee. Most blocks pay by day three.
2. **THE CORE** — Salvaged reactor-adjacent hardware from the fallout years, retrofitted to run the district grid. Nobody's supposed to own one privately. Korvo does.
3. **NEON WARD** — Years after the fallout, some residents came back changed — thicker skin, stranger reflexes, no complaints. The district stopped asking questions before it started asking for I.D.
4. **KORVO'S RISE** — He didn't build the district. He just bought the one thing that could turn it off.
5. **VESPER** — The drug of choice once the lights are back on: a shimmer-pink haze that shows up in every club window from Fifth to the Row. Nobody in the district asks what's in it, only who's pouring.
